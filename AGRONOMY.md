# Agricultural evidence — reviewed 2026-11-25

## Regional field yield defaults
[New England Vegetable Management Guide](https://nevegetable.org/cultural-practices/estimating-vegetable-yields), USDA NASS 2020–2024 averages, pounds per harvested acre. Convert to the generator's units per 1,000 net planted square feet by dividing by 43.56 and by the 0.72 planted fraction, then dividing by pounds per sale unit.

| Crop | lb/acre | Sale unit assumption | Units/1,000 net ft² |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Field tomato (specialty proxy) | 9320 | lb | 297.16 |
| Other peppers | 5820 | lb | 185.57 |
| Leaf lettuce (salad mix proxy) | 6020 | 4 oz bag | 767.75 |
| Garlic | 2300 | 2 oz bulb | 586.68 |

These are regional benchmarks, not farm-specific yield targets. Protected production needs separate assumptions: [New England protected tomato guide](https://nevegetable.org/crops/tomato-greenhouse-and-high-tunnel) reports approximately 1–5 lb/ft² depending on growing conditions and crop duration; do not treat a gross greenhouse court as covered growing floor.

## Planting quantities and timing
[University of Minnesota Extension crop and field planning](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/crop-and-field-planning-tools-for-vegetable-farmers). Approximate quantities per 100 feet of three-foot-wide planted bed; timings depend on variety and climate.

| Crop | Planting material | First harvest | Harvest duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cherry tomato | 75 transplants | 50–70 days after transplant | 3–4 weeks determinate; 8–10 indeterminate |
| Bell pepper (specialty proxy) | 150–170 transplants | 50–70 days after transplant | 6–8 weeks |
| Salad mix | 2,000–3,000 seeds | 25–45 days from seeding | 2–3 weeks |
| Garlic | ~600 cloves | about 10 months | one week |
| Day-neutral strawberry | 200–300 plants | 65 days from planting | 12–14 weeks field; 16 tunnel |

## Inputs needing local verification
- [Regional enterprise budgets](https://nevegetable.org/cultural-practices/crop-budgets) and [Cornell tunnel budgets](https://blogs.cornell.edu/hightunnels/economics/sample-budgets-spreadsheets/): validate crop labor, inputs, startup costs.
- [Maine direct-market price reports](https://extension.umaine.edu/agriculture/maine-farmers-market-price-report/): prices are market- and date-specific.
- [BLS agricultural wages](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/farming-fishing-and-forestry/agricultural-workers.htm): national median wage is not a loaded local labor cost.
- [UMN irrigation source sizing](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/digging-or-expanding-a-well): 1 inch/week is a preliminary design assumption, not crop-specific need.
- [NRCS cover crop standard](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/cover-crop-ac-340-conservation-practice-standard): use state-specific technical guidance and actual soil testing.
