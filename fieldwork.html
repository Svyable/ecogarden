04 / Enterprise Library

Make each enterprise earn its place.

Use a documented production benchmark and your own local prices and costs. The Missouri Extension laying hen budget uses 187 eggs per hen per year and approximately 0.25 lb feed per day. USDA NASS Honey, March 2026 reports 48 lb per producing colony in 2025. Neither guarantees local output.

All costs and prices are illustrative, not quotes. Include animal replacement, health, winter feed, mortality, packaging, processing, insurance and regulations as applicable. This is not a stocking or welfare recommendation. Do not double count revenue/costs already in another model.

05 / Farm Tycoon

Reinvest without running out of cash.

Test a phased expansion. The model first earns or loses the year's operating cash, then invests only available cash above the reserve, within the annual spending cap and land ceiling. New acreage earns its entered incremental margin starting next year.
