Energy landscape

Solar overlaps existing crop or roof area. No extra farmland is counted. Panel footprints, shade and spacing are conceptual only.

Incremental business case

Annual self-consumption is capped by total demand but not modeled hour by hour. Electricity export assumes permission and entered compensation; set export price to zero without an agreement. Crop revenue change is a uniform area-weighted sensitivity, not a yield prediction. No tax credits, financing, degradation, replacement, discounting or insurance included. Do not add savings to the main farm model if it already includes them.

Beyond photovoltaics

Rain capture is gross annual potential, not dependable water supply. Check tank size, timing, first flush, treatment, local rules and crop food safety. Battery runtime is a simple usable-energy estimate, excludes inverter power and losses, and does not guarantee backup functionality. A normal grid-tied array may shut down during outages.

Evidence and next-generation systems

UMass field trials found that crop outcomes varied with weather: yields were comparable in a hot, dry year, while full sun performed better in a warm, wet year. Test shade responses rather than promising universal yield improvements.
