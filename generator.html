Resilience lab

What if the harvest fails?

Stress-test the selected operating year without changing its blueprint. These are deterministic scenarios, not probabilities or forecasts.

Lost harvest and price reductions multiply. Direct input and labor costs do not automatically fall after a crop failure. The shock is charged once; fixed costs are unchanged. No insurance proceeds, animal revenue or automatic financing assumed. Incomplete base budgets produce incomplete stress tests.

Water & labor

Can you actually operate this farm?

Water is an editable sizing scenario for all built crop beds, not a crop-specific irrigation prescription. Outdoor effective rain is credited; protected crops receive no rainfall credit. Assumes uniform application over the month. Excludes animal drinking water, wash/pack, cover crops, evaporation from storage, peak daily flow, restrictions and water-source capacity. Storage is illustrative, not a pond or tank design. Labor excludes unmodeled animals and other work.

Regenerative transition

Plan the next decade

Preserve perennial blocks, infrastructure and habitat; rotate annual blocks, including protected courts, through different plant families and cover crops. Choose cover mixes adapted to your local climate and termination method. This is a transition sketch, not a guaranteed soil improvement trajectory.

Feasibility notes

All revenue and input-cost coefficients are illustrative USD per 1,000 net planted ft², not measured yields or local prices. Field/raised blocks assume 72% planted space; protected courts assume 65%. A 10% annual reserve is applied to modeled crop infrastructure capital. Taxes, debt, land purchase, animal expenses and major site works are not included. This engine compares heuristics; it does not certify optimality.
