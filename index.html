03 / Seasonal operations

The year, not just the harvest.

Use site-specific frost dates and weather, protected-culture planning, crop rotation, soil tests and irrigation design. An unheated tunnel does not guarantee winter growth; cold-season harvest depends on plant establishment and light. No year-round heated greenhouse is assumed.

04 / Coastal market strategy

Sell the harvest before planting the field.

Westport · Tiverton · Little Compton

Test premium demand with chef samples, standing weekly orders, subscription pickup, carefully graded bouquets and seasonal botanical collections. Treat affluent-household demand as a hypothesis, not a customer count. Prioritize direct sales where labor and logistics justify the price premium.

90-day validation gate

Days 1–30: interview 15 households and 10 chefs or florists; collect competing prices and parcel quotes. Days 31–60: trial a small crop block and obtain paid or conditional recurring orders. Days 61–90: compare realized price, pack-out, hours and repeat purchases; commit to the first tunnel only after credible demand and a feasible parcel.

Track weekly sales per planted bed-foot, paid labor minutes per sale unit, shrink, repeat orders and gross contribution per outlet. Expand the best-performing enterprise, not the most photogenic one.

05 / Site due diligence & evidence

From concept to an actual farm.

Before leasing or purchasing

Check surveyed boundaries, agricultural use and farm-stand rules, legal access, wetlands and buffers, flood exposure, wind, salt spray, soil contamination and drainage, irrigation source and water quality, electric capacity, packing sanitation, insurance and delivery routes. Verify tunnel permits, snow/wind design and setbacks with the town; do not assume one acre receives an agricultural zoning exemption.

Use a lease contingent on site suitability and permissions. Install drip irrigation, bed infrastructure and basic wash/pack first; phase tunnels and cooler only as customers justify them. Public visitor access and value-added food processing require separate review.
